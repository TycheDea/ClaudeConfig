## 3. cand_2 ESCALATE clearance — **GRANTED**

cand_2 entered at v2 ESCALATE 3.14% open_dark, judgeable only on an explicit 1:1
albedo examination clearing the open-dark signal as spec'd content rather than
painted shading. Frames examined at 1:1, full-res, albedo channel:
`oak_dark/cand_2/studio_albedo/macro_00.png`, `macro_01.png`, `macro_02.png`,
`macro_03.png`, `gameplay_00.png`, `gameplay_03.png`, and
`oak_dark/cand_2/ship_albedo/macro_01.png` (byte-identical read to the studio
albedo, confirming the channel is lighting-independent as expected).

**Clearance granted.** The open-dark region is base colour, not multiplied
shading. The decisive observation is in
`oak_dark/cand_2/studio_albedo/macro_01.png` (and its `ship_albedo` twin): the
gilt tracery lines run continuously from the top of the arch — where the
surrounding wood is comparatively light — down into the deepest near-black
ground, and hold **constant gold value across that entire transit**. A painted
contact shadow or baked AO is multiplicative: it would attenuate the gilt as it
enters the dark zone. It does not. The dark ground itself is a flat near-black
field with faint vertical grain and no gradient ramp hugging the arch jamb, the
imposts, or the niche corners — the places a painted occlusion term would
concentrate. `macro_02.png` shows plain oak at even luminance with no baked
gradient, and `macro_00.png` (the mushiest frame in the set) shows irregular,
non-directional value patches with no light/dark edge pairing — smear, not
shading. This matches the spec'd "deep near-black brown" oak with a shallow
empty niche.

For contrast, the two defects this test is meant to catch **are** present in the
other candidates and were found by the same method: the radial spill around the
painted flame in `oak_dark/cand_1/studio_albedo/macro_00.png`, and the fixed-
handedness edge pairs in `oak_dark/cand_3/studio_albedo/macro_00.png`.

  in cand_1.

  Consistent with `normal_lap_std 0.107` vs the reference row's `0.288` and
  `normal_flat_frac 0.277` vs `0.015` (`prop_audit_cand_*.txt`).
- **Flat roughness.** `roughness_mean 1.000, roughness_std 0.000, metallic 0`

  the three lighting arms.
- **Blind tell disconfirmed.** A painted-shading tell was named blind against

- **ship_beauty hue wrap.** `stats_cand_*.txt` reports h≈282–359 on several
  `ship_beauty` rows; this is the known 0/360 wrap on dark pixels and carries no
  visual meaning. Frames were judged directly.
