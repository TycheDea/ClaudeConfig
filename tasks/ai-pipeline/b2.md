# Phase B2 — Environment Light + Ground (subplan)

Source: `zesty-bubbling-acorn.md`, Plan B / Phase B2, planned 2026-07-23.
Amended same day at the GPU go-ahead: the user ruled **"Go — but regenerate
fixtures too"** — both A-phase fixtures are regenerated against the locked
direction and the better of old-vs-new is kept (selection protocol below).
Puts the locked Castilian direction (`docs/visual-quality.md` §A,
user-approved AS-IS at the B1 gate — VQ-A4's numbers are targets, not
proposals) into the running game: the dusk-plateau HDRI becomes the default
environment, the ground sets go Castilian, fog and the ground palette retune
to match, and the directional light aligns with the HDRI's baked sun.
**Scope boundary — B2 does NOT touch:** props or zone dressing (B3 — the
candelabra_shrine placement and rock/tree props in zones.ron stay
byte-identical), the parked east zone's resurrection (B3), characters (B4),
VFX RONs / telegraph constants / any palette code beyond `zone_palette()`
(B5), and no renderer-feature work.

This file breaks the phase into 7 single-agent tasks plus the gate. The
orchestrator dispatches one agent per task, verifies with the given command
(never prose), commits on green, and strikes the checkbox. Order is the
listed order and it is load-bearing: B2.1 (review-tool flag) first; **B2.2
(HDRI regen + selection) before every ground review** — B2.3/B2.4 render
under the *winning* environment, so the sky decision must already be made;
B2.5 (swap) after both ground decisions; B2.6 (deletions) after the swap
(old assets must be unreferenced before they go); B2.7 (light) last — its
constant derives from the winning HDRI manifest.

Tiers (campaign routing table): **fable** = art-direction judgment + real
integration risk (fog/palette value choices anchored to measured data; the
sun-alignment mapping from equirect azimuth to engine world space); **sonnet**
= ordinary script/RON/test edits and the generation runs (prompt/reseed
judgment included — A1.6/A2.10 precedent); nothing here is haiku-worthy.
Old-vs-new **vision selection is the orchestrator's** (fable with full
context — A2.8 precedent), recorded in the Selection log below.

**Shell discipline (all tasks): FOREGROUND commands only** — background
shells die silently on this box (A2 lesson). Generation runs get an explicit
generous timeout (~10 min), never `run_in_background`.

## Regeneration ruling + selection protocol (user go, 2026-07-23)

User ruling at the GPU go-ahead: **"Go — but regenerate fixtures too."**
Both incumbents get one fresh challenger each, generated against the locked
§A text. Protocol, applied by B2.2 (HDRI) and B2.3 (cracked earth):

- **What varies, and why** (fact 11: identical seed+prompt would reproduce
  the incumbent byte-for-byte — a no-op regeneration would be theater):
  - *cracked_earth challenger:* retuned prompt **and** fresh seed. The
    incumbent's final prompt ("cracked dry earth, parched mud flats, brown
    dusty soil, deep fissures") predates the lock and dropped "sun-bleached"
    along the way; the challenger prompt restores the locked VQ-A1/A4
    language — starting point: `"cracked dry earth, sun-bleached parched
    clay, warm dusty stone tones, deep fissures"` — seed 2 (≠ 0/1, both
    consumed by A1.6's attempts).
  - *HDRI challenger:* fresh seed only (starting point: seed 11 ≠ 7), same
    prompt. The incumbent prompt already IS the locked VQ-A5 mood verbatim
    ("dusk sky over an empty dry Castilian plateau … low amber sun near the
    horizon"; B1 changed no wording that bears on it) — inventing prompt
    drift here would vary something the lock says is already right. The
    worker may fold in locked-§A wording only to fix a concrete defect the
    incumbent shows (none known), and must say so in the manifest.
- **Selection semantics:** challenger and incumbent are reviewed
  side-by-side under identical conditions (same render tool, same angles/
  size, same environment — the ground reviews both render under the winning
  HDRI; the HDRI review renders the same DamagedHelmet turntable for both).
  The orchestrator judges against the VQ rules (VQ-A1/A2/A3 for ground;
  A2.8's criteria — seam, equirect geometry, IBL read, VQ-A1/A5 register —
  for the sky) and records the verdict in the Selection log below. **If the
  incumbent wins, it stays and the ruling is satisfied** — regeneration
  happened; selection is evidence-based. A challenger that fails its own
  machine gates (tiling/seam/self-checks) after the reseed allowance loses
  by default.
- **Winner mechanics (swap rule — replaced fixture deleted, not archived):**
  winners keep the incumbent PATHS — `content/textures/ground/cracked_earth/`
  and `content/textures/env/castilian_plateau_dusk_2k.hdr` — so zone refs,
  the B2.5 swap, and B2.6's repoints are identical either way. A winning
  challenger's files *replace* the incumbent's in place (old files gone in
  the same commit); a losing challenger lives only under `target/` and is
  simply not promoted. The kept asset's `generation_manifest`/`.manifest.json`
  and its CREDITS row always describe the ACTUAL kept asset (seed, prompt,
  date) — B2.2/B2.3 update the row on replacement, touch nothing on a hold.
- **Downstream data flow:** B2.5's fog anchor and B2.7's sun constant
  (az/el/tint) are read from the winning HDRI's on-disk manifest AFTER
  B2.2 lands — never from this file's incumbent numbers (fact 10 note;
  B2.7 brief).
