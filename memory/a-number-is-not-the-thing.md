Fires when reporting a property verified from a number, writing a numeric acceptance band, or reading a gate that cleared while the artifact looks unchanged.

**Why:** a nearest-vertex distance blind to sign reported buried geometry as "nothing penetrates"; a composite cleared its ≥0.25 statistic while no visible pixel moved (mean-of-variation can't tell "detail everywhere" from "detail in a few places"); a yaw fit with a 180° near-symmetry compared front silhouettes against back silhouettes and manufactured a headline result; an A/B's floor covered neither the metrics nor the subjects its verdict cited; an island-percentile pre-screen stayed green (1.61×/0.40%) across a chalky albedo patch a judge flagged at first 1:1 read, and the fix worker's "37-texel residual" audit only searched its own diagnostic's region, so the loudest artifact — of a different origin — was invisible to it (artifact: `docs/reviews/town/arch-retess-round3-2026-08-05.md`, gate red through a fix round).

**How to apply:**
- Before reporting verified, ask what the metric is physically incapable of detecting, and confirm it separates pass from the specific failure claimed absent. A distance metric is not a containment test.
- Measure what ships (evaluated mesh, composed sidecar, rendered frame), not an upstream snapshot.
- Write the must-be-a-no-op control before the experimental run.
- A gate passing is necessary, never sufficient — look at the frames. A statistic clearing wide while the image is unchanged indicts the metric, not the image. A percentile/histogram gate is structurally blind to a small localized patch — eyeball the frames at 1:1 (orchestrator or render worker) before spending a judge.
- A residual accounting derived from the fix's own diagnostic only sees the defect family that diagnostic searched for; the clean claim needs a full-artifact independent scan ([[instrument-cannot-grade-itself]]).
- A fit reported as an extremum (best yaw, argmin) must emit the winner's margin over its nearest rival as a raw field; a margin within the claimed effect means the fit cannot adjudicate that claim, and a structural near-symmetry makes the margin unfixably small.
- Before writing an acceptance band, confirm a good artifact can physically satisfy it; a band no good input meets makes "the measurement improved" evidence of damage.
- An anchor or baseline must be producible by the current process and selected the same way as the candidates: a gate anchored on an artifact of a since-deleted stage, or a winner-selected baseline compared against unselected rolls, indicts the comparison, not the candidates — and a metric must not count spec'd content as defect (artifact: `docs/reviews/props/era-attribution-2026-08-05.md` — 1.78× delit anchor + survivor-selected "4/6 clean" + dark-frac counting spec'd soot produced 0/11 false BLOCKs).
- Pre-registered floors must cover every metric and every subject the verdict will cite, or those claims are pre-declared visual-only in writing.
